# Domain terms

- Market scope: Proudmoore connected-realm non-commodities or US-wide commodities. Never interchange these prices.
- Source scan time: upstream updatedAt; distinct from local fetch time and report generation time.
- Return on cost: projected net profit divided by purchase cost. This differs from margin on revenue.
- Regional sales estimates: TSM aggregate data; not observed Proudmoore sales or a guarantee of a personal sale.
- Watchlist: research candidates without permission to buy or a recommended quantity.
- History: shortlisted price observations only. Listing disappearance does not establish a sale.
